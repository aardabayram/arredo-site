Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Arredo ("the app") is operated by Scoutli Teknoloji ve Yazılım A.Ş. ("Scoutli", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information Arredo processes, why, and the choices you have. Questions: arda@scoutli.co.

The short version

  • You don't need an account. Arredo identifies your device with a random identifier that is not linked to your name or email.
  • Photos you choose to redesign are sent to our AI partners only to create your design, after you agree to it in the app, and are deleted from our servers within 24 hours.
  • Your designs are stored on your device.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't show ads and we don't track you across apps.

Information we process

Room photos

When you start a design, the photo you choose (camera, library or a sample) is resized, stripped of location and other metadata on your device, and uploaded to our servers on Cloudflare. It is sent to our processors fal.ai and OpenAI only to generate your design. We delete the uploaded photo when the design finishes and in any case within 24 hours; generated images are kept on our servers for up to 7 days so your device can download them. We ask fal.ai to expire files after one hour; OpenAI may retain API data for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring and does not use it to train models. We never use your photos to train AI models.

Designs

Your before and after images are saved in the app on your device. They are removed when you delete them, use "Delete My Data", or delete the app.

App identifier, credits and purchases

We create a random identifier stored on your device to keep track of your credits and jobs. Purchases are processed by Apple; we use RevenueCat to validate purchases and manage subscription status. We receive purchase details such as product, price, dates and transaction identifiers — never your payment details.

Device integrity

We use Apple's DeviceCheck to prevent abuse of the free design. It doesn't give us any identifier from your device.

Usage analytics and diagnostics

We use Phase to understand how the app is used (for example, which screens are viewed and whether a design succeeded), together with device model, OS version, language and approximate region derived from IP address. We use Sentry to receive crash reports and performance diagnostics. Neither includes your photos or contact details.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your device's push token to tell you when a design is ready.

How and why we use information

To provide the app and your designs (performance of our contract with you), to process purchases and credits, to prevent fraud and abuse and keep the service secure (legitimate interests), to understand and improve the app (legitimate interests), and to send notifications you enable (consent). Photos are sent to our AI partners only after you explicitly agree in the app.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that process it on our behalf: Cloudflare (hosting and storage), fal.ai and OpenAI (AI processing), RevenueCat (purchases), Phase (analytics), Sentry (diagnostics) and Apple (purchases, DeviceCheck, notifications). We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention

Uploaded photos: deleted after processing, at most 24 hours. Generated images on our servers: up to 7 days. Job records: 30 days. Purchase and credit records are kept as long as needed for accounting and to prevent abuse, without any personal contact information.

Your choices and rights

Use Settings → "Delete My Data" to delete your designs on the device and your data on our servers (purchase and credit records are kept as described above). Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK or California law), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. Contact us at arda@scoutli.co; you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States, the European Union and other countries. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Children

Arredo is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We'll post updates on this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes will be highlighted in the app.
