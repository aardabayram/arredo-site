We're a small studio and we read every message. Email us at arda@scoutli.co — include the User ID shown at the bottom of Settings so we can find your account faster.

Frequently asked questions

How do credits work?

Each design uses one credit. New devices get one free design. Arredo Pro Weekly includes 20 designs per week and Arredo Pro Yearly includes 60 designs per month; unused plan credits reset at the end of each period. Subscribers can buy credit packs, which never expire.

A design failed — did I lose a credit?

No. If a design can't be finished, the credit is refunded automatically.

How do I restore purchases on a new iPhone?

Open Settings in Arredo and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings in Arredo and tap Manage Subscription, or go to the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

How do I get the best results?

Shoot from a corner with the lights on, hold your phone level, and capture as much of the room as you can.

How do I delete my data?

In Arredo, open Settings → Delete My Data. This deletes your designs on the device and your data on our servers. You can also email us.
